# SVG clipping, V3a: `clipPath` (17 November 2026)

SVG lane, RFC 0009 V3a. The parent session approved splitting V3 into V3a
`clipPath`, V3b gradients and patterns, V3c masks, and V3d markers and
`use`; the split is recorded as an amendment in RFC 0009's migration plan.

## What landed

- **`div_svg::clip`.** `ClipPath` (units, transform, children, its own
  `clip-path`), `ClipChild`, `ClipContent` and `ClipReference`, plus
  `clip_content`, which paints content clipped by a resolved `clipPath`:
  - a path-based clip when the content reduces to one path, anti-aliased;
  - otherwise a mask: the content in a layer, the clip's coverage
    composited onto it with `DstIn`, chained through nested `clip-path`s.
- **Ported (BSD).** `core/paint/clip_path_clipper.cc`:
  - `PathBasedClipInternal`, including the nested-clip intersection and
    its 500-verb limit (`ClipPathNestedRasterOptimization` is stable in
    147);
  - the mask chain of `PaintClipPathAsMaskImage`;
  - the fill box as the reference box of `url()` on an SVG element.
- **Clean-room.** Blink's resource clipper and `clipPath` element are LGPL
  and were not opened. The content transform, which children count, how
  they unite, the mask's coverage and layers, and reference cycles were
  written from SVG 2 and CSS Masking, then fitted to Chromium's pixels.
- **`tools/svg/paint_corpus.py --set v3`.** 54 cases, each at scale 1 and at
  real-zoom scale 2 (108 captures). Each case's ids are prefixed with its
  index, since ids are page-wide. Sets v1 and v2 are unchanged.
- **Fixtures.** `tests/fidelity/fixtures/svg-paint-v3/`, one capture per OS
  (about 320 KB). The Linux and Windows sheets differ only in
  `child/text`.
- **`crates/div-svg/tests/paint_corpus.rs`.**
  - It gains `paint_v3_matches_chromium_147`.
  - Its harness resolves `clip-path` references, with cycles broken at
    the closing reference, and paints groups with their `opacity` and
    `clip-path`.
  - Gap lines may start with `linux` or `windows`.

## What the corpus taught

These are measurements against Chromium 147.0.7727.50. Each rule below was
kept because it made cases exact and the alternatives tried did not.

- **Uniting children.**
  - Several children are united in one `OpBuilder` build.
  - Uniting them one `Op` at a time leaves `union/mixed-rules` 6 pixels
    off.
  - Appending them into one path is worse for every fill type.
  - A mask for mixed rules is worse still, as is a mask for every
    multi-child clip.
  - A single child's path is used as it is. Simplifying it breaks the
    star polygons.
- **The content transform.** With no nested `clip-path`, the canvas
  applies the `clipPath`'s transform and its `objectBoundingBox` map, and
  the path stays as it is. Transforming the path first leaves
  `units/obb-circle` 2 pixels off at scale 2.
- **Cycles.**
  - A self reference, or `c` to `d` to `c`, is broken at the reference
    that closes the cycle. This is consistent with `SVGResource`'s
    per-client cycle cache (BSD) when the target's reference is checked
    first.
  - That unresolved nested reference rules out the path-based clip, and
    the mask chain stops at it.
  - Treating the closing reference as "no clip-path" instead leaves
    `edge/cycle` 75 pixels off.
- **Mask layers.**
  - The content layer is clipped to the bounds of the `clipPath`'s
    content. The mask is not clipped.
  - Skia's scan conversion depends on the clip a path crosses. Without
    the bounds clip, the red stroke's edge in `edge/self` and `edge/cycle`
    is a few levels off.
  - With the mask clipped as well, the circle's edge in `edge/cycle` is
    off by up to 38 levels.
  - Clipping to the union or the intersection of the chain's bounds, or
    outsetting the bounds by a pixel, is worse.
- **Mask coverage.** Each child is drawn opaque, as the shape painter draws
  it (`drawRect`, `drawOval`, else the path). Children with
  `display: none` or `visibility: hidden` are left out. Stroke, `fill`,
  `fill="none"` and `fill-rule` are ignored. `clip-rule` and
  `shape-rendering` inherit from the `clipPath`.
- **Edges.**
  - An empty `clipPath` clips everything.
  - A missing reference, or one to an element that is not a `clipPath`,
    leaves the element unclipped.
- **Opacity.** `opacity` on a clipped group wraps the clip: the clipped
  content is drawn into the opacity layer. The other order leaves
  `edge/opacity` off.

## For engine item E4

`clip-path: url()` on HTML elements and the `div-style`/`div-paint`
wiring are engine item E4, after E1. It can call `clip_content` with a
resolved `ClipPath`. These facts carry over:

- the reference box of an SVG element is its fill box;
- references resolve with cycles broken at the closing reference;
- the opacity layer wraps the clip.
