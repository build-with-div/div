# SVG `use` and `symbol`, V3d part 1 (19 November 2026)

SVG lane, RFC 0009 V3d. V3d lands in two PRs: this one (`use` and
`symbol`, including `use` inside a `clipPath`), then markers.

## What landed

- **`div_svg::use_element`.**
  - `UseAttributes` holds a `use` element's `x`, `y`, `width` and `height`.
  - `translation` gives the placement after the `use`'s `transform`.
  - `instance_viewport` gives the viewport of a `symbol` or `svg`
    instance.
  - The module doc records the rules for building the instance tree, which
    is the caller's (DOM) work.
- **`div_svg::viewport`.**
  - `ViewportAttributes` and `NestedViewport` describe a nested viewport:
    initial values, the content transform and the content size.
  - `is_empty_view_box` detects a zero-size `viewBox`, which disables a
    nested viewport.
- **`div_svg::clip`.** A `use` of a shape in a `clipPath` is a
  `ClipContent::Shape`. Only `text` stays unsupported.
- **Clean-room.** Blink's `use` element and its layout container are LGPL
  and were not opened. The behaviour comes from SVG 2 and black-box
  observation of Chromium 147. The empty-`viewBox` rule is from Blink's BSD
  `svg_container_painter.cc`, which was already in `out\chromium-ref`.
- **`tools/svg/paint_corpus.py --set v6`.** 61 cases, each at scale 1 and
  at real-zoom scale 2 (122 captures). Sets v1 to v5 are unchanged.
- **Fixtures.**
  - `tests/fidelity/fixtures/svg-paint-v6/` holds one capture per OS
    (about 300 KB). The Linux and Windows sheets are byte-identical.
  - `svg-paint-v3/known-gaps.txt` loses its two `child/use` lines, which
    are now exact.
- **`crates/div-svg/tests/paint_corpus.rs`.**
  - It gains `paint_v6_matches_chromium_147`.
  - Its harness builds the instance tree and paints a `use` as a
    container with its own placement.
  - It paints `symbol` and `svg` instances, and nested `svg`s, through
    `NestedViewport`.
  - It paints group `opacity` as cc plays it back (below).
- **`tests/support`.** `inherit` in a presentation attribute keeps the
  parent's value, and `Element` is `Clone`.

## What the corpus taught

These are measurements against Chromium 147.0.7727.50.

- **The use's translation is its own transform.** A `clip-path` or `mask`
  on a `use` with `x` and `y` is in the translated user space
  (`effects/clip-user`, `effects/mask`).
- **Cycles drop only the closing `use`.** A `use` whose target is the
  `use` itself or one of its ancestors renders nothing; a copy counts as
  its original. In `edge/mutual` the outer instance still paints its
  rect.
- **The viewport, not the `viewBox`, clips a symbol.** `symbol/meet`
  shows content outside the `viewBox` inside the viewport. The symbol's
  own `width` and `height` apply when the `use` sets none
  (`symbol/own-size`).
- **`use` in a `clipPath`.** A `use` of a shape clips as the shape. A
  `use` of a `g` or a `symbol` clips everything out.
- **Group opacity as cc plays it back.** These cases are the first to
  have `opacity` on content that is not a mask or clip, and both measured
  effects come from cc's playback (BSD `PaintChunksToCcLayer` and
  `PlaybackFoldingIterator`):
  - The `SaveLayerAlpha` is bounded by the union of its content's
    integer drawable bounds. Unbounded, one circle edge in `use/opacity`
    differs by 4 levels at scale 1.
  - A layer holding a single drawing operation is folded into it, with
    the operation's float alpha multiplied. Painted as a layer instead,
    every pixel of `use/twice`'s faded rect is one level off.
  - `div-paint` applies opacity at the call site and already folds this
    way (`paint_record.rs`). The harness models the fold for colour
    paints only.

## Remaining gap

`chart/legend` is Recharts' legend icon: two half-circle arcs of radius
5.33, which is within a float ulp of half the chord. It differs at the
stroke's inner edge where the arcs meet, by 49 pixels (up to 43 levels)
at 1x and 46 at 2x, the same on both OSes. Throwaway diagnostic captures
(Windows, `out\svg\diag`, not committed) narrowed it down:

- The same path drawn directly, without `use` or `symbol`, differs the
  same way. So does it with no fill, with round joins, and without the
  joining line. Its differences reach 50 levels at device scale 1 and 5
  at 2.
- `div-svg`'s conics for these arcs are bit-identical to skia-safe's
  `arcTo`.
- Centring the arc exactly on the chord's midpoint, as SVG's
  implementation notes do for scaled-up radii, changes no pixel.

The cause is unmeasured. Chromium 147 pins Skia `abbe599f`, while
rust-skia's m147 checkout is at `2b5c647f`. A comparison of their `arcTo`
and stroker is pending; it needs Skia sources, which the standing fetch
rule does not cover. Owner: SVG lane (path geometry), as a follow-up.
